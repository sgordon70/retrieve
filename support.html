Common questions

How does scheduling work? Retrieve automatically decides when to show you each card again based on how you grade it (Again / Hard / Good / Easy) — no setup required.

Is there a free trial? Yes — you can create one deck with up to 15 cards and use every feature, including image occlusion and search, before deciding to unlock the full app with a one-time purchase.

Does Retrieve sync between devices? Not yet — this is planned for a future update.

Can I get my data out of Retrieve? Yes — every deck can be exported to CSV at any time from the deck's "More" menu.
